# Chosun campus facade reference guide

Reference-only photographs; no texture license asserted. This is visual evidence, not a dimensional survey.

## IT융합대학 — map 2026 #30, detail ID 138
- Official floor directory: 10 above-ground levels.
- 10-floor rectangular slab with strongly continuous turquoise glazed horizontal ribbons and narrow white floor bands; denser vertical white supports through lower facade. Low 3-4-floor annex to one end; flat roof with raised rectangular rooftop screen and small projecting canopy at end. White/light-gray and pale blue-green glazing.
- Caution: Official department also uses an annex; do not depict faculty as one detached generic cube.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/138/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554797749064100.jpg

## 공과대학 제1공학관 — map 2026 #57, detail ID 134
- Official floor directory: 17 above-ground levels.
- 17-floor tall shallow rectangular slab. Dominant vertical white piers/fins run nearly full height in close regular rhythm, separating green-gray window stacks. Light warm-gray wall edges, flat roof, raised end rooftop service blocks. Much taller and more vertical than IT building.
- Caution: Building is 제1공학관, not entire College of Engineering.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/134/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554798083056100.jpg

## 공과대학 제2공학관 — map 2026 #36, detail ID 150
- Official floor directory: 5 above-ground levels.
- 5-floor long red-brown brick wings meeting at angled/L court. Narrow white horizontal floor-edge bands. Repetitive pairs of blue-green rectangular windows with light lintels; external open stair landings or balcony stack at left end, white flat slabs. Flat roof and low parapet.
- Caution: Separate from 17-floor 제1공학관; dorm tower visible behind in reference must not become part of this building.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/150/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554798168712100.jpg

## 글로벌하우스(기숙사) — map 2026 #34, detail ID 142
- Official floor directory: 11 above-ground levels.
- 11-floor white residential tower group above low white podium. Towers are narrow slabs, with dark olive/gold inset vertical balconies and sparse horizontal black slit windows on end walls. Oversized flat roof caps with long cantilevered edges. Podium contains broad turquoise glazing and central tall glazed entry bay; some warm wood-toned entry accents.
- Caution: Image shows multiple linked towers, not one solid slab. Official dorm site says 11 above-ground and 2 basement levels.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/142/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1555046339547100.jpg

## 미술대학(미술관) — map 2026 #15, detail ID 135
- Official floor directory: 8 above-ground levels.
- 8-floor circular/polygonal drum. White body, dark continuous window ribbons, tall cylindrical exterior posts projecting outside perimeter. Bold red, yellow, blue and charcoal color blocks/bands on facade and posts. Flat roof with open railing/parapet. Small green-tinted fan/arched glass entrance canopy.
- Caution: Use downloaded official photo as palette authority; some older photos show whiter or mural-clad versions. Map 14 sculpture workshop is separate low auxiliary structure.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/135/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554798680094100.jpg

## 백학학사(기숙사) — map 2026 #07, detail ID 147
- Official floor directory: 9 above-ground levels.
- 9-floor light-gray/white curved or kinked residential wings. Dense regular small punched paired windows. Tall dark turquoise glazed stair/elevator strips mark wing ends. Shallow recessed balconies in central portion and broad dark open lattice canopy projecting above center. Low red-brown podium/terrace with arched green translucent entry canopy.
- Caution: Source photographs may predate 2026 Glocal Baekhak renovation. Preserve basic massing; current facade details not independently verified.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/147/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554798758407100.jpg

## 사회과학ㆍ사범대학 — map 2026 #04, detail ID 136
- Official floor directory: 6 above-ground levels.
- 6-floor elongated white rectangular block distinguished by giant multi-floor round-top arches on front and end faces. Slim deep white piers frame recessed horizontal window rows. Wide thin flat gray roof projects strongly beyond walls. Recessed open/shadowed top band under roof; avoid ordinary punched-window-box treatment.
- Caution: Official building label 사회과학ㆍ사범대학; 2026 handbook calls 법사회대학(사회과학)·사범대학. Education science departments also occupy 자연과학관; faculty is not identical to one building.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/136/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554798885430100.jpg

## 서석홀(평생교육원,박물관) — map 2026 #08, detail ID 141
- Official floor directory: 4 above-ground levels.
- 4-floor beige/peach square tower at one end of asymmetrical 2-floor low wing. Flat stepped roofs. Tall narrow turquoise glass stair strip joins tower to wing. Tower has sparse small square windows; lower wing uses larger dark glazing and broad colonnaded entrance recess. Broad stone plaza staircase in front.
- Caution: Do not confuse image-search photos of curved white hall with this confirmed campusMap reference. Official label includes lifelong education and museum.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/141/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554799320404100.jpg

## 중앙도서관 — map 2026 #27, detail ID 127
- Official floor directory: 7 above-ground levels.
- Complex light-gray/white library. Official photo shows low elongated front wing raised on round white pilotis, with two upper window bands in narrow vertical modules and an open shaded ground floor. Stepped higher rectangular rear volumes rise behind, with rooftop fin/screen. Flat roof and strong projecting horizontal fascia.
- Caution: Official floor directory enumerates 7 floors for whole building, but photo front wing only about 3 visible levels. Do not extrude whole footprint uniformly to seven floors. Glass pyramid elsewhere on campus is not reliable library identification.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/127/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554802666144100.jpg

## 학생회관(동아리연합회) — map 2026 #10, detail ID 145
- Official floor directory: 6 above-ground levels.
- 6-floor long rectangular student union. Alternating red-orange brick vertical piers and light concrete columns, prominent white horizontal slab bands, broad dark-blue rectangular window panels. Simple flat roof; shallow rectangular entrance canopy on slim supports and ground-level full-height glazing. Tree-lined paved forecourt.
- Caution: Photo is tightly framed and some uppermost levels outside view; official directory enumerates 6.
- Page: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/campusMap/chosun/145/detailView.do
- Photo: https://csu.chosun.ac.kr/sites/chosun/atchmnfl/building/temp_1554802948307100.jpg
