## Corrections

1. Central Library #127: Removed arbitrary horizontal y=-220 division which raised lateral low wings to seven levels. Tall mass now follows the narrower north stem of mapped footprint; low three-level body retains its footprint and open round-column ground level. Added dense narrow window rhythm instead of generic paired-office windows, strong but thin fascia, roof terrace rail and green roof recreation surface. Added the clearly documented blue-glass pyramid and original geometric mullions. Removed generic rooftop equipment from this building.
2. International Building #146: Four-floor generic masonry model replaced with the official five-floor contemporary building. Added green curtain glazing, overhanging white perforation-pattern screen, large lettering, projecting rectangular glass bays, round support columns, horizontal sunshades, end fins and distinct roofline. The adjacent 'global' building is identified by location and the official first-floor Global Studies occupancy; it is not the remote Global House dormitory.
3. Physical Education #132: Removed the oversized 65%-width central rooftop box and tall 12×10 green cuboid. Broad flat roof now has thin rounded outer nosing and a shallow small lightwell. Added top lancet/pointed arch windows, separate horizontal upper glazing strip, tall subdivided glass bays, piers and continuous fascia, based on the close-up and aerial.
4. Business College #133: Corrected four floors to eight from the official floor directory. Added long white facade piers/belts and stepped forecourt/entrance forms supported by the newly inspected official photo.
5. Global House #142: Retained mapped podium/two-tower arrangement, added projecting roof caps, warm olive recessed balcony bands and thin balcony slabs/rails seen in the official photo.
6. Seoseok Hall #141: Added tall narrow teal stair-glazing panel and broad stepped entrance approach.
7. Engineering II #150: Added the visible projecting open end-landings and slender guard rails.
8. Student Hall #145: Emphasized alternating brick piers and broad pale slab bands.
9. Art College #135: Added original triangulated green fan-shaped entrance canopy and ribs.

## What is observed versus estimated

Observed features: identity of the two adjacent buildings; 5/7/8 official floor counts; presence/type of pyramid, pilotis, screen, green glazed bays, sunshades, gym roof edge and top pointed windows, dorm caps, stair glazing and canopies.
Estimated: exact heights, screen hole count/diameter, hidden elevations, facade orientation within footprint, projection depths, pyramid dimensions, staircase counts, roof court striping, furnishing positions. This is source-informed exterior reconstruction, not a measured survey and not a claim that every current detail is exact.

Original mapped footprints, coordinate transform, 62 building entries and 47 destination identities are retained. Height changes for #133 and #146 alter their collision heights consistently. Main building and original site source files are untouched. Road continuity and transit assets are supplied by separate modules.

Reference photos are research-only and must not be embedded or redistributed as model textures. All mesh vertices, facade marks, lettering and materials are original procedural geometry. Before source/model snapshot is in before/; reproducible close views in before_render/ and after/.
