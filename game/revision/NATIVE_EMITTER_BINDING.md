The placement contract preserves the fixture head center. The authoritative final native manifest supplies native_light_emitter_offset_m. Runtime passes the corresponding emitterOffset option only to optional shadowless spotlights and their distance selection. Poles, bases, mesh transforms and render-only ground patches do not move.

At final assembly use verify-lighting-release.py native_options_bytes(manifest SHA, augmented native SHA, manifest offset) to generate dist/street-lighting-native.js. The verifier requires exact generated bytes and final identity. PENDING_FINAL_NATIVE placeholders cannot pass final verification.
