## Stairs and terrain integration
The central 108 stair has 108 actual treads, a wider lower flight, three intermediate landings, continuous stainless side rails/posts, granite nosings/joints and a flush memorial strip. Its 108 total is established; exact width, metric rise/run and distribution across landings are estimates. Side granite cheeks are not represented as a verified wheelchair ramp.

Photo-supported approaches for Business College, Seoseok Hall, Hwanggeumchu, Business Incubator and Medical III have separate treads, landings and rails. Their riser counts adapt to the final approximate ground surface so endpoints connect. Hwanggeumchu's photographed side ramp and Workshop's straight external metal stair are distinct geometry. Exact counts and slopes are unmeasured.

navigation.json includes upward-facing walkableSurfaces and pedestrianPaths in Blender Z-up coordinates. Solid building collision boxes remain separate. Terrain is cut beneath actual footprints and stair polygons, and foundation/retaining skirts close ground/base mismatches. Main-building coordinates and the original 47 destination IDs remain intact.

## Evidence and limits
The official campus pages and images are enumerated in source_data/architectural_review.json. Individual images were actually opened during research and implementation. Most reference image URLs date to earlier campus photography, frequently 2019; this is not a claim of perfect 2026 as-built coverage. Model geometry, frames, rails, letters and materials are original procedural work, with no reference photographs embedded as textures.
