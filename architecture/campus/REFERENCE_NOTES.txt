Original work
All architectural shell/facade meshes, vegetation, generic contemporary cars, furniture and materials were generated originally for this project. No downloaded model geometry, photo textures, logos or photographs are embedded. Procedural materials are shared for practical web rendering. Source photos were inspected only as visual references and must not be redistributed as textures.

Explicit approximations
Terrain elevations, floor-to-floor dimensions, unphotographed facade sides, window counts and spacing, rooftop equipment, accessory structures, exact tree species/positions and current car positions are approximations. Official directory floors represent the highest listed level of the complex, not every wing. Central Library is modeled as a low three-level front wing with pilotis and higher rear volume; IT has a low annex and taller glass-band slab; Global House has a podium and two towers; Seoseok Hall has a low wing and taller end. The Education arches, Art cylindrical color-banded facade and Engineering 1 ribs are photo-informed original geometry. Natural Science's mapped inner courtyard is cut through the mass/roof. No inaccessible interior or floor plan is claimed to be verified.

Near-main reconciliation
Main footprint/rotunda OSM features are omitted because the original detailed main model is authoritative. Original front/rear parking, stairs, trees and cars are retained where compatible. Old single ground mesh is replaced by continuous campus terrain; old plants/cars that overlap mapped new buildings are culled. The near-main road layout remains an approximate locally reconciled connection rather than a claim of current surveyed geometry.

Recreation
Run build_campus.py in Blender with mesh_architecture.py and facade_specials.py in the same folder. Input source JSON paths are declared near the top; original site.blend and site_builder.py are used for original shared near-main assets. navigation.json holds landmark arrival positions, road paths, bounds and conservative footprint collision boxes. campus_coverage.json documents per-building sources and confidence. A main-building model and source/site directory are required separately for a full recreation.
